- First-fruits sprite sheet was drawn for WALL. Five 96×64 cells depict a fictional pilgrim moving a basket beside a schematic altar. The action follows selected motions in Mishnah Bikkurim 3:6. https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Bikkurim.3.6
- `bar-kosiba-letter.png` is an original four-cell sprite sheet. Each 96×44 cell illustrates the selected plant groups in P. Yadin 57. The baskets are teaching illustrations. The historical letter establishes a request. https://www.posenlibrary.com/entry/simeon-bar-kosibas-order-four-species
- `reservoir.png` contains two original 128×76 schematics of the Mamluk and Ottoman reservoir phases under Wilson’s Arch. They draw on the written description of Stratum 1. https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/Report_Detail_Eng.aspx?id=25662
- Historical photograph, Library of Congress Matson Photograph Collection, LC-DIG-matpc-14762. The catalogue records no known restrictions on publication and dates the original negative to approximately 1900–1920. https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2019704965/
- `western-wall-2016.jpg` is Wikimedia Commons' 960×640 rendition of “Holy Land 2016 P0709 Western Wall”, photographed by Fallaner on 24 March 2016, under CC BY-SA 4.0. WALL places a separate outline over the displayed photograph during the exercise. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Holy_Land_2016_P0709_Western_Wall.jpg https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- `bergheim-1865.jpg` is the Library of Congress 640×509 rendition of Peter Bergheim's “Exterior of the Haram-Ash-Shárif. Detail of masonry at wailing place”, LC-USZ62-100162. The photograph appeared in the 1865 Ordnance Survey of Jerusalem, plate 14b. The British Library records 1864–1865. The Library of Congress reports no known publication restrictions in the U.S. The work was published in 1865. https://www.loc.gov/item/90710514/ https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-003237471 https://hdl.loc.gov/loc.pnp/cph.3c00162
- Atlas land backdrop rendered for WALL from Natural Earth's public-domain 1:110m land polygons, vector release v5.1.2. The plate covers longitude −88° to 100° and latitude −1° to 69°. It depicts physical coastline, with teaching reference points added by the game. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/110m-land/
- Kotel objects, 5 by 2 sprite sheet with 64 × 48 cells. Original pixel drawings created for WALL as educational representations of the objects and setting.
- Everyday life objects, 4 by 2 sprite sheet with 64 × 48 cells. Original pixel drawings created for WALL as educational representations.

- `culture-objects.png` is a WALL-original 256×96 sprite sheet of eight 64×48 cells. Its stone, books, memoir, forms, and recording icons represent object types. They are illustrations, with no transcribed signatures, census details, archival photographs or original audio.
- `geniza-letters.png` is a WALL-original 192×96 teaching sprite sheet. Each row has a front, a back and a schematic evidence view. The illustrated paper and handwriting marks do not reproduce scans or letter text. Chapter 15 links to the Princeton Geniza Project records for the original documents.
- `study-circle.png` contains six original 64×48 portraits of fictional participants. The second row depicts responses within the conversation. The characters are not portraits of the authors whose works are linked.
- `baku-archive.png` is a WALL-original 384×168 sprite sheet of 96×56 cells. The building, booklets, photograph mount and map are teaching illustrations. Linked archives hold the historical photographs and records.
