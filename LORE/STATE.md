WALL has 24 chronological chapters and the prologue, 35 community atlas entries, 20 Kabbalah readings, 20 calendar observances, eight responsibility records and 409 source catalogue entries. The four locales are Turkish, English, Hebrew and Azerbaijani. The Rabin case in v0.8.6 distinguishes the Tel Aviv rally and mourning, Yigal Amir’s opposition to the Oslo talks, and Clinton’s response. Luna reviewed the claims and translations against ACC08A–C.

v0.8.10 follows Laitman’s Russian-language book in 1984, the 1991 founding of Bnei Baruch and later multilingual teaching. Luna checked the dates and source roles in all four translations. B07 is the university record and English abstract. B07A links the author’s copy of the 2015 Russian journal article. Its opening pages identify the 2007 Hebrew source and the chronology comes from p. 193. The new exercise asks the player to credit the academy for its account of its courses and Meir for the earlier publication. Players can save an example applying those distinctions to another community.

In the downloaded web ZIP at 320 CSS pixels and 200% text, Chromium verified keyboard entry, touch submission and draft recovery after reloading in all four languages. Axe-core reported 20 passed checks and zero violations under its WCAG 2 and 2.1 A/AA tags on the open study dialog in each locale. The lesson module gained 1,550 gzip bytes and the catalogue 297. No Kabbalah module was requested before opening the workshop in these runs.

v0.8.9 fixes a stale-tab save that erased another tab’s completed chapter. Completing a chapter now writes an independent record, and opening a saved scene records any legacy completions that lack one. Navigation, retry and portable export combine stored and pending completions. The entry view updates the Continue destination when another tab saves a scene. Schema 1 exports contain one combined progress snapshot. In local Chromium, a real v0.8.8 tab overwrote the old snapshot after the new client had opened the saved campaign. Reloading v0.8.9 restored the two completions from the individual records. The three-engine regression also covers a blocked write, export, retry and reload.

Six headless Chromium samples per version at 320 × 700 produced startup medians of 193 ms and 196 ms in fresh contexts (v0.8.8 and v0.8.9), with ranges of 182–213 ms and 162–211 ms. Reload medians were 66 ms and 68 ms, with ranges of 45–81 ms and 54–90 ms. Both versions performed zero campaign writes at the entry view before the player made a choice.

Language and Settings work before entering. A valid chapter link or confirmed save import opens the scene directly, and a locked chapter link opens the entry view. Opening that view leaves campaign progress untouched. In six fresh Chromium runs per version at 320 × 700, startup went from 412 canvas fill calls and one campaign write in each v0.8.7 run to zero of both in each v0.8.8 run. The first scene renders after the entry choice.
